# Payments

## Statuses

Mollie has seven payment statuses; the interface has six.

| Mollie                       | Interface         | Why                                                                                  |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `open`, with a checkout link | `requires_action` | The payer must be sent to the link; it is the payment's `redirectUrl`                |
| `open`, without one          | `pending`         | An off-session charge Mollie has not settled yet                                     |
| `pending`                    | `pending`         | Awaiting the bank                                                                    |
| `authorized`                 | `authorized`      | Funds held, awaiting capture                                                         |
| `paid`                       | `succeeded`       |                                                                                      |
| `failed`                     | `failed`          | `failure` from Mollie's `failureReason` (cards) or `bankReasonCode` (SEPA)           |
| `canceled`                   | `canceled`        |                                                                                      |
| `expired`                    | `canceled`        | Mollie's own wording: the payer abandoned it, or an authorization was never captured |

`failure.code` is normalised from Mollie's reasons: `insufficient_funds`, `card_expired` → `expired_card`, the `invalid_*` card reasons → `incorrect_details`, the authentication reasons → `authentication_failed`, anything else → `declined`. The verbatim reason is in `failure.providerCode`.

## Amounts

Mollie takes amounts as strings with the currency's decimals: two for every currency it lists, none for `ISK` and `JPY` (Mollie: _Multicurrency_). The module converts from the interface's minor units by Mollie's table, not by the runtime's currency data, which disagrees for some currencies Mollie supports — Node's ICU gives `HUF` no decimals, Mollie two, so `{ value: 1050, currency: "HUF" }` is `"10.50"`. A currency Mollie does not list falls back to ISO 4217's digits.

## What `paymentMethod` may be

| Value              | Meaning                                                              | Mollie request                               |
| ------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| `cst_…:mdt_…`      | A saved method, from `ListPaymentMethods`                            | `sequenceType: recurring`, the mandate       |
| `tkn_…`            | A card token from Mollie Components, collected by your own card form | `method: creditcard`, `cardToken`            |
| a Mollie method id | The method your own picker chose: `ideal`, `creditcard`, `paypal`, … | `method`; the payer skips Mollie's selection |
| absent             | Mollie's hosted page lets the payer choose                           | no `method`                                  |

A saved method is charged as a Mollie recurring payment, which needs no payer, whether or not `offSession` is set. It belongs to one customer: charging it for another is `CustomerNotFoundError` for the method. `offSession` needs a saved method.

## Payer-present payments are redirects

Mollie has no payment that completes inside the creating call while the payer is present. `CreatePayment` without a saved method, and `CreateCheckoutSession`, both return `requires_action` with Mollie's checkout URL. `returnUrl` is therefore always required unless a saved method is charged.

`savePaymentMethod` (it needs a `customer`) makes the payment a Mollie `first` payment: once the payer completes it, Mollie creates a mandate for the customer, and `ListPaymentMethods` lists it. A Mollie mandate is listed when it is `valid` and may be used without the payer (scope `customer-not-present`); a card saved for one-click checkout only is not listed, because it cannot be charged off-session.

## Off-session charges settle later

A charge against a saved method is created `open` and settled by Mollie afterwards, so `CreatePayment` returns `pending`. The outcome arrives as a `payment.succeeded`, `payment.authorized` or `payment.failed` event, or through `GetPayment`. In particular a decline is a `failed` payment reported after the call returns: the interface's "a decline is a result, not an exception" holds, but the result is not in the creating call.

## Holding funds: `authorizeOnly`

Mollie can hold funds for later capture only for cards, Klarna, Billie, Riverty, Vipps, MobilePay, PayPal and Billink (Mollie: _Place a hold for a payment_). On a hosted page, a payer who picks any other method is charged at once. So:

- with a saved method, the mandate's method is read first, and a SEPA Direct Debit mandate is refused with a `PaymentError`;
- with a Mollie method id, any method outside that list is refused with a `PaymentError`;
- on a hosted page, the payer is offered only those methods.

Nothing reaches Mollie when a request is refused. A held payment is `pending` until Mollie reports it `authorized`. `CapturePayment` captures once: capturing less releases the rest, as Mollie does for cards. `CancelPayment` on an authorized payment releases it; Mollie processes a release asynchronously, so the payment may still read `authorized` for a moment.

## Refunds

Mollie requires an amount, so a refund without one refunds what Mollie reports as remaining. Mollie has no refund reason; the reason, and the idempotency key, are kept in the refund's metadata, out of what `Refund.metadata` returns, and in the description. Refund statuses `queued`, `pending` and `processing` are `pending`; `refunded` is `succeeded`; `failed` and `canceled` are `failed`.

## Cancelling

An `open` or `pending` payment is cancelled with Mollie's cancel endpoint while Mollie says it can be (`isCancelable`). A payment already `canceled` or `expired` is returned as it is, so a repeated cancellation is harmless. Anything else is `InvalidPaymentStateError`.

## Errors

- **Not found** is Mollie's 404, and an identifier of the wrong shape.
- **State errors** (`InvalidPaymentStateError`) come from the module's own reading of the payment before it calls Mollie: capturing what is not authorized, refunding what is not paid or more than remains, cancelling what has settled. A refusal from Mollie itself is never read as one. Mollie answers 422 for a request it will not process but also, per its documentation, when the account's payment services are suspended; a 422 is a `PaymentError`, so an account problem is never reported as "this payment cannot be refunded".
- Everything else (401, 429, 5xx, the network) is a `PaymentError` carrying the HTTP status (and the field Mollie names) in `providerCode`.

## Idempotency

Mollie honours `Idempotency-Key` on every POST, for **one hour**, and answers 400 when a key is reused with other parameters (Mollie: _API idempotency_).

- `RefundPayment` and `CapturePayment` replay at any time. The key is stored in the refund's or capture's own metadata, and a call first looks among the payment's refunds or captures for one made under it: a replay returns the original refund, or the payment as captured, even after the payment's state no longer allows the operation, and even after Mollie has forgotten the key. A partial refund replayed after the hour is not made twice.
- `CreatePayment`, `CreateCheckoutSession`, `CreateCustomer` and a release replay within the hour, by Mollie's own key handling. After the hour, Mollie treats the key as new: the provider's limit, which the module does not widen for these.
- `CreateSubscription` replays at any time (see [Subscriptions](./4.subscriptions.md)).

Mollie ignores the key on DELETE. Cancelling a payment, deleting a customer and detaching a method are DELETEs; a repeated cancellation returns the cancelled payment, while a repeated deletion or detachment reports the record as not found.
