# Subscriptions

Subscriptions are Mollie's own. Mollie keeps the schedule and creates each renewal payment against the subscription's mandate; this module maps the subscriptions interface onto Mollie's Subscriptions API, and reads Mollie's renewal payments back through the payment webhook.

## Creating

`CreateSubscription` checks the request with the interface's `ValidateSubscriptionRequest`, then:

1. reads the payment method's mandate: it must belong to the request's customer, be `valid`, and be usable without the payer. Otherwise the request is invalid (`InvalidSubscriptionRequestError`), and nothing is created;
2. looks for a subscription of this customer that was created with the same idempotency key, and resumes it if there is one;
3. otherwise creates the Mollie subscription;
4. without a trial or a billing anchor, charges the first period itself and records the charge on the subscription.

### The first period

Mollie creates subscription payments on the schedule's dates, after the call that made the subscription has returned. The interface wants the first period charged before `CreateSubscription` resolves. So without a trial or anchor, Mollie's schedule starts one period from today, and the module charges the first period as an ordinary recurring payment, carrying `antelopeSubscription` in its metadata. It is the subscription's `latestPayment` until Mollie's first renewal.

Mollie settles that charge after the call returns (see [Payments](./2.payments.md)), so the subscription is created `active` with the charge still pending. If the charge later fails, the subscription is `past_due` and `subscription.payment_failed` is raised. Mollie retries the payments it creates; it does not know about this one, so **a failed first charge is not retried**: the subscription stays `past_due` until Mollie's first renewal succeeds, or until you cancel it. If Mollie declines the charge in the creating response itself, the subscription is cancelled at Mollie, hidden from the interface, and `CreateSubscription` rejects with `SubscriptionPaymentError` — and does so again when its key is replayed.

### Replays

Mollie keeps an idempotency key for one hour. `CreateSubscription` does not depend on that: the key is stored on the subscription, and a replay at any later time finds the subscription and its first charge (looked up among the customer's payments) instead of creating or charging again.

A creation that fails partway — the network, Mollie, the process — **must be replayed with the same key.** The Mollie subscription may already exist, and Mollie will charge it from the next period. A caller that gives up instead, or retries under a new key, leaves a subscription that charges and that no one asked for.

## Time

Mollie's `startDate` and `nextPaymentDate` are calendar dates, and Mollie charges sometime on that day. The module reads them as days in Europe/Amsterdam (Mollie's documentation names no time zone):

- a **trial end** or **billing anchor** is rounded up to the start of the next Amsterdam day, so nothing is charged before the time asked for, and the subscription reports that rounded time as `trialEnd` and `currentPeriodEnd`. A time that already falls on an Amsterdam midnight is kept exactly;
- a **period** runs from one Amsterdam midnight to the next schedule date's midnight. Across a daylight-saving change, a period is an hour longer or shorter than whole days;
- month periods keep the anchor's day of the month and clamp short months, as Mollie does ("we will charge on the last date of the month").

## Intervals

| Interface   | Mollie      | Longest |
| ----------- | ----------- | ------- |
| `day` × n   | `n days`    | 365     |
| `week` × n  | `n weeks`   | 52      |
| `month` × n | `n months`  | 12      |
| `year` × 1  | `12 months` | 1       |

A longer period is refused (`InvalidSubscriptionRequestError`). The interval and its count are stored with the subscription, so a year reads back as a year.

## Statuses

| Situation                                                               | Status     |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| Mollie `active` or `pending`, before a trial ends, nothing charged      | `trialing` |
| Mollie `active` or `pending`, latest payment not failed                 | `active`   |
| Latest payment `failed`, `canceled` or `expired`; or Mollie `suspended` | `past_due` |
| Mollie `canceled` or `completed`                                        | `canceled` |

After a failed renewal, Mollie retries up to five times, then cancels the subscription; some SEPA reasons cancel it at once (Mollie: _Recurring payments_). A subscription Mollie ended that way reports `endReason: "payment_failed"`; one ended by a cancellation, by revoking its mandate or by deleting the customer reports `"requested"`. Revoking a mandate cancels every subscription that charges it, and deleting a customer cancels all of its subscriptions, at Mollie.

The raw Mollie status, start date, next payment date and interval are in `vendorData`.

## Cancelling

Mollie cancels a subscription at once, and a cancelled subscription can no longer be changed. Before cancelling, the module records on the subscription how and under which idempotency key it was cancelled.

- **`now`**: cancelled at Mollie; `canceled`, `endedAt` from Mollie, `endReason: "requested"`.
- **`period_end`**: cancelled at Mollie at once, so nothing more is charged, and recorded as ending when the current period ends. Until then the subscription reports its status and `cancelAtPeriodEnd: true`; from then on, `canceled` with `endedAt` at that period end. Asking again is not an error.
- **`now` after `period_end`**: the subscription can no longer be changed at Mollie, so the early end is recorded on the customer, in the customer's metadata beside your own (which is kept as it is).
- A repeated cancellation under the same key returns the subscription; under another key, on an ended subscription, it is `InvalidSubscriptionStateError`.

Changing the payment method of a subscription set to end at period end is refused with `InvalidSubscriptionStateError`: Mollie will not change it, and nothing will be charged again.

## Events

| Event                            | Raised by                                                 | Event id                               |
| -------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| `subscription.created`           | `CreateSubscription`, and its replays                     | `<subscription>:subscription.created`  |
| `subscription.canceled`          | `CancelSubscription` (`now`, or `now` after `period_end`) | `<subscription>:subscription.canceled` |
| `subscription.payment_succeeded` | the webhook of a paid charge                              | `<subscription>:<payment>:paid`        |
| `subscription.payment_failed`    | the webhook of a failed, cancelled or expired charge      | `<subscription>:<payment>:<status>`    |
| `subscription.canceled`          | the webhook of a failed charge after Mollie gave up       | `<subscription>:subscription.canceled` |

Mollie sends no webhook about a subscription itself, only about its payments. So:

- `created` and `canceled` from a call are raised in that call, to the handlers registered in that instance. A handler that throws makes the call reject; replaying the call's idempotency key raises the event again, under the same id.
- **A `period_end` cancellation raises nothing when the period runs out.** Nothing happens at Mollie then, and this module has no clock. Read the subscription, or act on `cancelAtPeriodEnd` and `currentPeriodEnd` yourself.
- A subscription Mollie cancels without a failed payment to report it (a revoked mandate, a deleted customer) raises no `canceled` event.

Payment events carry the subscription as read when the delivery is verified, which can be later than the state the event is about.

## Metadata

Mollie keeps about 1 kB of metadata per subscription. The module's own record (reference, idempotency key, interval, first charge, cancellation) takes up to about 400 bytes of it, which leaves a subscription's own `metadata` about 600 bytes. What Mollie does beyond that is not documented.

## Only this module's subscriptions

A subscription counts as this module's when it carries the module's own record in its metadata. Others on the account — made in the dashboard, by a plugin, or by other code — are not listed, are not found by `GetSubscription`, cannot be cancelled or changed through the interface, raise no events, and their payments are not marked with `antelopeSubscription`.
